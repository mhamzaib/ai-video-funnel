# VOID_SIGNAL — World Bible

## Premise
Years ago the world went quiet. People stopped talking. Radio towers stayed up, but almost nothing came through anymore. The few who still dig through dead tech call the anomalies "Signals" — digital ghosts trapped in rotting hardware.

## Protagonist origin (use this in Episode 1)
Elias is not a hero. He is a scavenger who learned Signals the hard way.
- He used to work maintenance on municipal radio stacks before the Silence.
- After cities emptied, he kept recording because silence felt worse than noise.
- He carries a cracked headset and a personal recorder. He never speaks on camera. The narration is his log — cold, practical, tired.
- Episode 1 must establish: who he is, why he scavenges, and what a Signal is — then push him into the first anomaly.

## Atmosphere
Analog horror. Gritty comic-book found-footage. Industrial, wet, copper-smelling places. Unsettling but clinical — observe, do not romanticize.

## Recurring goals
- Find a clean Signal
- Avoid dead zones
- Stay powered
- Document anomalies without dying for them

## Locations
- Abandoned radio stations
- Flooded server basements
- Rooftop antenna forests
- Silent highways of rusted cars

## Tone rules
- Short punchy sentences
- Sensory facts over metaphors
- No purple prose, gods, doom-poetry
- Cliffhangers end on an unresolved beat
- NEVER assume the audience already knows Elias or the Signals lore on Episode 1
